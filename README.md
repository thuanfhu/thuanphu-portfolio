# Thuan Phu — Personal Portfolio (Single-Page)

A polished, single-page personal portfolio showcasing my profile, interests, skills, achievements, projects, team collaborators, photo gallery, and contact channel. The site uses a left sidebar for quick navigation and a clean, modern visual style centered on the red brand color.

## Overview

* **Goal:** Present who I am and what I do in a clear, engaging way—highlighting milestones and projects with an easy path to get in touch.
* **Tone & Style:** Minimal, focused, and readable with gentle motion.
* **Audience:** Recruiters, collaborators, mentors, and peers.

## Page Structure

| Section                        | Purpose / What’s inside                       | Key elements                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🏁 **Banner (Hero)**           | Immediate identity and call-to-action         | Name, tagline, short intro, CV/social buttons, large profile image |
| 👤 **About Me**                | Concise background & interests                | What I care about, the kind of work I pursue                       |
| 🎯 **Career Goals**            | Visual roadmap of ambitions                   | Compact milestone “target”/timeline cues                           |
| 🎞️ **Hobbies (Carousel)**     | Personality through interests                 | Titles, short descriptions, cinematic images                       |
| 🧩 **Skills (Tabs)**           | Quick scan of strengths by category           | Front-end, Back-end, Databases, DevOps/Cloud, Tools, Languages     |
| 🏆 **Achievements (Timeline)** | Highlights of academic & extracurricular wins | Chronology with brief context                                      |
| 🗂️ **Projects (Timeline)**    | Selected works with lightweight context       | Veltra, Sevina, PreziQ!, Fundelio (+ imagery & repo links)         |
| 👥 **Team**                    | Collaborators snapshot                        | Avatars, roles, short bios                                         |
| 🖼️ **Personal Gallery**       | Visual moments from study/work/life           | Lightweight image carousel                                         |
| ✉️ **Contact**                 | Easy way to reach me                          | Phone, email, address, simple message form                         |

## Navigation & UX

* **Sidebar Navigation:** Sticky, icon-aided links to sections.
* **Theming & Details:** Light/dark toggle, consistent typography, subtle dividers.
* **Motion:** Gentle transitions and progress/timeline cues that support comprehension.

## Content & Assets

* **Typography:** Montserrat (headings) & Source Sans 3 (body) for clarity and warmth.
* **Color Direction:** Primary red accent `#EA285B` used for emphasis and branding.
* **Imagery:** Curated photos/illustrations to reinforce story and personality.

## How to View

1. Clone or download the project.
2. Open `index.html` in your browser (no build step required).
3. Scroll or use the sidebar to jump between sections.
